# Scientific material and assets

The figures and numerical results are from the manuscript **Beyond Isolated Corruptions: Benchmarking 3D Robust Perception across Compositional Adversity Space**. The manuscript PDF is not included in this repository.

- CARE architecture: manuscript Figure 4.
- Clean-trained robustness evaluation: original Figure 5, including all six radar plots and method legends. Metrics are averaged over applicable corruption types and severity levels. This is grouped with ComboGen.
- Qualitative detection results after the experimental tables: Figure 9.
- Single and compositional corruption sequence: Figure 2. The same camera–LiDAR scene is shown clean, with Rain alone, and with Rain followed by Fog. The last state is a composition, not isolated Fog. All six panels are original crops without synthetic changes.
- Corrupted camera and LiDAR examples: matched columns from manuscript Figure 3 at Severity 1, 3, and 5+. Original LiDAR 2× detail insets are preserved. Filename index 6 denotes the 5+ level.
- Table 7: original image from PDF page 12, nuScenes-Combo validation, free-form Fog + Echo Depletion + Occlusion composition, all 10 rows and 14 numerical columns.
- Table 8: original image from PDF page 13, KITTI-Combo validation, the same free-form composition, all 9 rows and 8 numerical columns.

The original table images omit the numbered paragraph captions and retain every column heading, method name, value, underline, boldface, and original row highlight. Table images are lossless crops of the PDF rendering. These CARE comparisons and Figure 9 are grouped under robustness enhancement. The paper PDF itself remains excluded.

Author names and contribution roles are retained; institutional affiliations and the Contact section are not displayed. The abstract is condensed for the webpage. Video positions are reserved and currently contain no videos.
