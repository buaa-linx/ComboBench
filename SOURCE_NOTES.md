# Scientific material and assets

The figures and numerical results are from the manuscript **Beyond Isolated Corruptions: Benchmarking 3D Robust Perception across Compositional Adversity Space**. The manuscript PDF is not included in this repository.

- CARE architecture: manuscript Figure 4.
- Detection teaser and enlarged detection figure: manuscript Figure 9.
- Corrupted camera and LiDAR examples: matched columns from manuscript Figure 3 at Severity 1, 3, and 5+. Original LiDAR 2× detail insets are preserved. Filename index 6 denotes the 5+ level.
- Table 7: nuScenes-Combo validation, free-form Fog + Echo Depletion + Occlusion composition, all 10 rows and 14 numerical columns.
- Table 8: KITTI-Combo validation, the same free-form composition, all 9 rows and 8 numerical columns.

Blue table rows identify CARE variants (-C). They do not indicate that every value in that row is the best. Em dashes indicate unreported values.

Author names and contribution roles are retained; institutional affiliations and the Contact section are not displayed. The abstract is condensed for the webpage. Video positions are reserved and currently contain no videos.

Icon provenance and license notices are in `assets/icons/SOURCES.md`.
