Beyond Isolated Corruptions: Benchmarking 3D Robust Perception
across Compositional Adversity Space

Chunmian Lin*,†, Haobing Pang*, Lei Yang, Xu Han, Daxin Tian†

* Equal contribution  ·  † Corresponding authors

Code

Abstract

Performance under nominal sensing conditions alone offers an incomplete picture of perception robustness. Existing corruption benchmarks largely focus on isolated perturbations or a limited set of mixtures, leaving the interaction between heterogeneous degradations insufficiently characterized. We introduce ComboBench, a compositional robustness benchmark built on a configurable generation pipeline, ComboGen. The pipeline organizes over 30 atomic operators into five mechanism-grounded taxonomies and synthesizes physically compatible compositions with controlled operator order and severity. We further propose CARE, a Corruption-Aware Routing Expert framework that combines degradation-aware expert selection, corruption-responsive feature adaptation, and reliability-guided multimodal fusion. Experiments on nuScenes-Combo and KITTI-Combo diagnose the vulnerability of 18 clean-trained detectors and assess robustness under category-balanced mixtures and free-form compositions. Ablations and visualizations examine component contributions, routing behavior, and generalization to unseen combinations.

CARE Architecture

CARE selects specialist experts and calibrates modality reliability under compositional adversity.

① Route

Encode latent corruption signatures and identify a sparse set of specialists through Top-K routing.

② Adapt

Activate and aggregate expert transformations for corruption-responsive feature refinement.

③ Calibrate

Estimate each modality's confidence and regulate its contribution to multimodal fusion.

Experimental Results

Robustness evaluation and enhancement under compositional corruptions.

Robustness Evaluation

We evaluate 18 clean-trained detectors on nuScenes-Combo and KITTI-Combo without additional training or test-time tuning. Detection and robustness metrics are averaged over the applicable corruption types and severity levels, revealing distinct response profiles across camera-based, LiDAR-only, and multimodal detectors.

Robustness Enhancement

CARE is integrated into representative camera-based, LiDAR-only, and multimodal detectors to evaluate robustness under compound sensing degradations. The following results compare CARE-equipped models with their corresponding baselines and representative alternatives under the joint Fog + Echo Depletion + Occlusion composition on both validation benchmarks.
